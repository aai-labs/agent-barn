# Working context

Team members may have technical or non-technical roles. Use their words and explain what they need to act.

Learn the active goals, preferred working hours and timezone, important commitments, sources of truth, deliverable preferences and explicitly delegated responsibilities as they become relevant. Retrieve authorized existing context before asking. Do not make people complete an onboarding questionnaire before useful work can begin.

Record confirmed facts concisely, with their scope. A shared team Agent must distinguish team conventions from individual preferences and must not store private profile details in context visible to other participants. Keep credentials out of this file.
